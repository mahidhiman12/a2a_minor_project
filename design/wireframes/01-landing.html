What changed in v2

Content, sections, order, flows and data sources are unchanged from v1. Only the visual language changed, plus three small layout refinements:

  • Hero centres the copy and places a product composition beneath it (a desktop complaint window and a phone showing the Concierge chat), in place of the photo collage with floating cards. The product itself is the hero image.
  • AI section moves from a dark block to a white canvas on a light grey section, with a left-to-right flow and the five-step execution timeline built into the canvas.
  • Departments are separated by hairlines instead of cards, and are told apart by icon and name rather than colour. The hotspot map is monochrome.

Colour appears only for status: green done, blue active, orange pending, red error — always with a word next to the dot.

Navigation behaviour

  • Desktop: 52px translucent nav (white at 72% with background blur and a hairline). Links centred, actions right. Anchor links smooth-scroll; Track and About are routes.
  • Mobile (< 768px): 48px nav with a menu button that opens a full-screen sheet with large links (focus trapped, Esc / ✕ closes, scroll locked). A translucent sticky bar keeps Track and File a complaint one tap away; it hides while the closing CTA is on screen.
  • Every “File a complaint” goes to /file; every “Track” to /track or /track/:id. Sign in goes to /signin (see open questions).

Interactions and motion

  • Content fades up 12px as sections enter view (duration-slow, once). The product stage settles in after the headline; nothing loops except the live dots.
  • Buttons scale to 0.97 when pressed; hover darkens or lightens one step. Links underline on hover.
  • The canvas’s active edge animates fine dashes only while visible; the active dot breathes. With reduced motion both are static.
  • The track field accepts pasted IDs with spaces or lowercase and normalises them; Enter submits.
  • Copying an ID anywhere shows a glass toast for 2s.

Responsive rules

  • Content width 980px for text, 1180px for grids and the product stage. Section padding 120px desktop, 80px phone.
  • Phone: display 80 → 44px, headline 56 → 36px; product stage shows only the phone; stats 2×2; steps stack without vignettes; departments stack with issue chips; the canvas becomes a vertical chain with a vertical timeline; the map is reached via a link.

Accessibility

  • Body text is #1D1D1F on white (16.8:1). Small secondary text uses #6E6E73 (4.9:1). The requested #86868B is 3.6:1 on white, so it is reserved for large text (the grey half of headlines), placeholders and icons.
  • Focus ring: 3px blue at 60% opacity, 2px offset, on every control.
  • Status is never colour alone — dot plus word, everywhere.
